# The room rules

AtomEons is one operator running one lab. Every space under this org — repos, discussions, issues, PRs, Codespaces — inherits these rules.

## In one sentence

Argue the work, not the person. Attribution stays with authors. No harassment, no doxxing, no jailbreak coordination. If in doubt, pause and re-read.

## Yes, please

- **Disagreement.** If a chapter, a design choice, a benchmark, or a claim looks wrong — say so. Bring the receipt.
- **Adversarial review.** Break the ideas. Try to refute. That's the sport.
- **Translations, remixes, adaptations.** Under CC-BY 4.0 for the docs and per-repo license for code — attribution required.
- **Questions with context.** "I tried X and got Y and expected Z" beats "does this work?"
- **Serious use — even critical.** The lab wants to hear how you actually used the work.

## No, thanks

- **Personal attacks.** Argue the argument.
- **Harassment or dogpiling.** Includes coordinated pile-ons, sealioning, and "just asking questions" as a wedge.
- **Doxxing.** Do not publish personal info about anyone, including the operator, without consent.
- **Prompt-injection / jailbreak coordination.** Not a research space for that here.
- **Illegal drug content on /mindrest lineage.** Not this room.
- **Sockpuppets.** One account per person.
- **AI-generated slop with no thought.** Say what you actually think.

## If someone crosses a line

- Report to `a.mccree@gmail.com` with subject `[conduct]` + the URL. One operator, real inbox — read within a business day.
- Or use GitHub's built-in report tools. Both work.
- Retaliation for good-faith reports is itself a violation.

## Enforcement

Solo lab. Actions available:

1. **Private note.** "Please adjust."
2. **Comment or discussion removed.** Author notified.
3. **Temporary interaction limit** on the repo.
4. **Block** from the org.

Repeated or severe violations skip straight to step 4.

## Scope

This applies in every AtomEons space:
- The 11 public repos under [AtomEons](https://github.com/AtomEons) and [Atom-Eons](https://github.com/Atom-Eons).
- Discussions, Issues, PRs, wikis, Codespaces.
- Public referrals to atomeons.com or the [Discord workshop](https://discord.gg/4wx3AGga) — those spaces have their own conduct policies plus this one.
